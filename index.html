    Race to the Spice Islands

    Split the class into two crowns. Portugal sails east around Africa; Spain sails west across the Atlantic and Pacific. Each round, both teams answer the same question (on whiteboards or through a team captain). You mark each team right or wrong, and the ships sail. Right answers bring fair winds and streak bonuses; wrong answers still move, just slowly.

    Race to the Spice Islands
    0%
    0%

    What really happened: The race was real. Portugal reached the Spice Islands first, in 1512, by sailing east around Africa. Magellan's Spanish fleet arrived from the west in 1521. Both crowns claimed the islands, because no one could measure longitude well enough to tell which side of the Tordesillas line they were on. In the Treaty of Zaragoza (1529), Spain gave up its claim in exchange for 350,000 ducats.
